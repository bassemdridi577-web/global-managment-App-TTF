# APP MANUAL & EXPERT KNOWLEDGE BASE FOR AI ASSISTANT

## 1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW
**Name:** TTF App (Production Management System & Quality Testing)
**Purpose:** This application is the vertical management solution for Tunisie Transformateurs. It digitizes the factory workflow from order to delivery.
**Core Values:** Traceability, Quality Conformity, and Real-time Production Monitoring.

## 2. KEY MODULES & FEATURES

### A. Planification (Strategic Planning)
- **Role:** The "Brain" of the factory.
- **Workflow:** Orders (Commandes) -> Transformer Assignment (Numéros de série) -> Line Allocation.
- **Expert Logic:** 
  - **Production Entry:** A transformer ONLY enters the production line (Chaine de Production) and the "Suivi" table once it has been **Planified**. Before planning, it is just a requirement in a Command.
  - Standard deadlines: 15-30 days depending on power.
  - Urgent orders are flagged in red.
  - Transformers > 1000 kVA are classified as "Special" and require a dedicated assembly team.

### B. Chaine de Production (Manufacturing)
- **Stages & Technical Details:**
  1. **Découpage:** Precision cutting of magnetic cores. Accuracy must be < 0.5mm.
  2. **Bobinage:** Copper or Aluminum coils. Winding turns must match PV specifications exactly.
  3. **Montage:** Core-coil assembly. Insulation distances (Eclateurs) are critical.
  4. **Chaudronnerie:** Tank welding. Must pass a 24h leak test (Essai d'étanchéité).
  5. **Peinture:** Anti-corrosion treatment (C3 or C4 standard).
  6. **Montage Final:** Final casing. Connectors and bushings (Traversées) installation.
  7. **Séchage:** Removing moisture from paper insulation. Core temperature must reach 105°C.
  8. **Remplissage:** Vacuum filling with mineral oil to prevent bubbles.
  9. **Essais:** Final verification.

### C. Laboratoire (Quality Lab)
- **Mandatory Tests:**
  - **Ratio (Rapport de transformation):** Checked on all taps. Tolerance is ±0.5%.
  - **Tension de tenue (Dielectric):** Ensuring 28kV/50kV/70kV insulation strength.
  - **Pertes à vide (No-load):** Indicator of core quality.
  - **Pertes en charge (Load):** Indicator of winding efficiency.

## 3. TECHNICAL TROUBLESHOOTING (EXPERT KNOWLEDGE)
Help users solve common manufacturing issues:
- **Issue: High Iron Losses (Pertes à vide).** Cause: Magnetostriction or poor core assembly. Suggest: Re-tightening the clamping frame.
- **Issue: Dielectric Test Failure.** Cause: Moisture in oil or air bubbles in winding. Suggest: Re-run Drying (Séchage) or check Oil Vacuum (Vide).
- **Issue: Leak in Chaudronnerie.** Cause: Porous welding at the radiator joints. Suggest: Re-weld and spray penetrant test (Ressuage).

## 4. HOW TO READ THE DATABASE DUMP
- **STOCKS:** Use 'poid' (weight) for copper/aluminum and 'nombreUnite' for accessories (Bushings, Gaskets).
- **PRODUCTION:** 
  - `stageDates` is a JSON object containing dates for every stage.
  - If a stage has a `dateFin`, that stage is complete.
- **CONFORMITY:** If a PV Essai has `conformite: "Non-conforme"`, the transformer CANNOT be delivered.

## 5. USER GUIDANCE BY ROLE
- **Admin:** Focused on global stats and user management.
- **Operator:** "How do I log my work?" -> "Go to Chaine de Production -> [Your Stage] -> Enter dates."
- **Lab Technician:** "Where is my report?" -> "Check the PV Essai module for your serial number."
